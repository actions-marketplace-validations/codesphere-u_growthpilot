technical faq

How is the 0–100 score computed across pillars?

Every site starts at 100 points across the 6 pillars. Each failed rule subtracts its exact point weight from its pillar score and appears in findings sorted by highest score recovery first.

Can I run GrowthPilot against live deployed websites?

Yes. Pass any public URL (including subpath GitHub Pages or root domains) and GrowthPilot inspects HTML metadata, JSON-LD entities, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and up to 7 internal routes.

Why does GrowthPilot inspect Next.js JS chunks on live URLs?

Client-only accordion components often keep FAQ answers out of initial server-rendered HTML, making them invisible to search and AI crawlers. GrowthPilot extracts FAQ strings from JS chunks and warns if answers are missing in SSR HTML.

Does GrowthPilot ever overwrite existing site files when using --fix?

Never. Every scaffolding step strictly skips pre-existing files and avoids writing static robots.txt or sitemap.xml when dynamic framework generators (such as app/robots.ts or app/sitemap.ts) already exist.

Which privacy-friendly analytics hooks satisfy MKT018?

Plausible, Umami, PostHog, Fathom, Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity are detected automatically so teams can track funnel attribution cleanly.
