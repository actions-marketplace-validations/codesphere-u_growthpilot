About GrowthPilot & Verified Proof

GrowthPilot was built because small product teams repeatedly launch sharp software, then spend months invisible in Google and AI answer engines due to missing llms.txt files, broken canonicals, and generic hero copy.

Most SEO and marketing suites lock basic technical audits behind $99–$499 monthly subscriptions, slow cloud crawls, and opaque scores. GrowthPilot gives builders a deterministic single-file engine you run locally in under a second with zero external dependencies.

Project Goals

Rather than guessing what search engines or AI answer engines want, every check in GrowthPilot is inspectable, reproducible, and forkable under the MIT License.

Run one command in your terminal, wire it as a stdio MCP tool inside Claude Code, Cursor, or Antigravity, or block broken pull requests cleanly in GitHub Actions with SARIF 2.1.0 annotations.

Under the Hood: 6 Scoring Pillars

  • Technical SEO (20 / 20 pts) — Title length, meta description, HTTPS canonical URL, valid sitemap.xml, single <h1>, and image alt attributes.
  • GEO & AI Search (20 / 20 pts) — Valid llms.txt & llms-full.txt, permissive GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot rules, and SSR FAQ visibility.
  • Structured Data (15 / 15 pts) — Archetype-aware Schema.org JSON-LD @graph and PWA manifest.webmanifest.
  • Social & Viral Graph (15 / 15 pts) — OpenGraph cards and Twitter/X summary_large_image metadata.
  • CRO & Copy Clarity (15 / 15 pts) — Active CTA verb, quantified buyer proof, and zero AI filler words.
  • Launch & Growth (15 / 15 pts) — Channel playbook, programmatic SEO matrix, and analytics hook detection.
