## Competitor & Workflow Comparison Angles

- **GrowthPilot vs. Monthly SEO Suites:** Free open-source CLI emitting real code (`HEAD_TAGS.html`, `SEO_SCHEMA.jsonld`, `llms.txt`) instead of view-only dashboards.
- **GrowthPilot vs. Generic LLM Prompts:** Reproducible 18-rule deterministic scoring (`MKT001`–`MKT018`), SSR vs. JS-chunk FAQ detection, and zero fabricated URLs.
- **GrowthPilot vs. Lighthouse Only:** Adds GEO (`llms.txt` + `GPTBot`/`ClaudeBot`/`PerplexityBot` checks), Schema.org `@graph` generation, CRO anti-slop scoring, and multi-channel playbooks.
